# UDCT v7.14 — Exploratory Hessian and Phase-Space Audit

Status: Exploratory research archive — not formal preregistration.

## Overview

This repository preserves the UDCT v7.14 exploratory investigation of QUMOND-related Hessian behavior, Jeans-equation consistency, and phase-space admissibility in dwarf-galaxy models, with particular attention to Hydrus I.

The purpose is to document exploratory calculations, identify potential mathematical and physical consistency boundaries, and provide materials for independent reproducibility checks.

- Reproducibility package: Python, CSV, and JSON materials intended to support independent checks.

Scientific Scope and Limitations

These exploratory investigations concern model-internal mathematical and physical consistency. They do not establish observational confirmation of UDCT or demonstrate superiority over standard MOND, QUMOND, GQUMOND, or dark-matter models.

Calculations using observationally motivated Hydrus I inputs must be distinguished from direct fitting of individual stellar line-of-sight velocity measurements.

The archived exploratory results are not equivalent to a completed observational validation.

Reproducibility

Readers should consult the archived PDF and reproducibility package for the available model definitions, computational inputs, scripts, numerical outputs, and documented limitations.

Reproduction of any individual result requires verification of the corresponding inputs, numerical settings, and implementation.

Registration Status

This repository documents exploratory work completed before formal preregistration.

Formal UDCT v7.14 preregistration of new hypotheses, acceptance criteria, and prospective tests remains a separate future step.

Previously explored results must not be presented as newly preregistered predictions.

Research Principles

- Explicit physical admissibility criteria
- Separation of exploratory and confirmatory testing
